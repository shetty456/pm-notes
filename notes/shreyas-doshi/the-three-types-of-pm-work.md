# The Three Types of PM Work

**Source:** Shreyas Doshi on Twitter / various talks

## The Framework

Shreyas breaks PM work into three buckets most PMs don't consciously separate:

### 1. Upstream Work
Work that happens **before** the team starts building.

- Strategy
- Roadmap decisions
- Framing the problem correctly
- Stakeholder alignment

**This is where the highest leverage is.** Most PMs underinvest here.

---

### 2. Downstream Work
Work that happens **during and after** building.

- Reviewing designs
- Attending standups
- Writing specs
- Launch coordination

This is what most PMs think their job is.

---

### 3. Overhead Work
Work that **must happen** but doesn't directly create product value.

- Meetings without clear outcomes
- Status updates that could be emails
- Reporting

---

## The Key Insight

> "Great PMs spend 60–70% of their time on upstream work. Average PMs spend 60–70% on downstream."

If you're always reacting, you're always downstream.

---

## How to Apply This

- Audit your last 2 weeks — which bucket did your time fall into?
- Protect upstream time with calendar blocks
- Delegate or eliminate overhead where possible
