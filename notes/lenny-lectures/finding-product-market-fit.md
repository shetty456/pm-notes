# Finding Product Market Fit

**Source:** Lenny's Podcast — Rahul Vohra (Superhuman)

## The Core Idea

PMF is not a binary switch. It's a spectrum. The question Rahul Vohra asked:

> "How would you feel if you could no longer use this product?"

The options: Very disappointed / Somewhat disappointed / Not disappointed.

**If 40%+ of users say "Very disappointed" — you have PMF.**

Superhuman was at 22% when they ran this. They used this to get to 58%.

---

## How to Move the Number

1. **Segment** — Who are the "very disappointed" users? What do they have in common?
2. **Ask them why** — What's the primary benefit they get?
3. **Double down on that benefit** — Don't try to please everyone.
4. **Ignore the "not disappointed" group** — Stop trying to convert them.

---

## Key Insight

The instinct is to fix churned users. The better move is to **obsess over your best users** and clone them.

Most teams spend 80% of time on detractors. Flip it — spend 80% on your power users.

---

## Takeaways

- PMF is measurable, not just a feeling
- Segment before you optimize
- The 40% benchmark is a north star, not a guarantee
