# The Art of the Roadmap

**Source:** Lenny's Newsletter

## What a Roadmap Is NOT

- A promise
- A project plan
- A list of features sorted by who asked loudest

## What It IS

A **communication tool** that signals priorities and direction — not a contract.

---

## The 3-Bucket Framework

| Bucket | Time | Focus |
|--------|------|-------|
| Now | This quarter | Committed, scoped |
| Next | Next quarter | Directional, not locked |
| Later | 6+ months | Themes, not features |

---

## Common Mistakes

- **Too much specificity too early** — teams ship to roadmap, not to outcomes
- **No "why" behind each item** — stakeholders push back when they don't understand rationale
- **Roadmap ≠ backlog** — don't confuse them

---

## The Principle

> "A roadmap should be a bet on outcomes, not a list of outputs."

Tie every item to a metric it moves. If you can't, it shouldn't be on the roadmap.
